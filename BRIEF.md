# ALL SORTED — Product Brief
*Generated: 2026-03-28*

## Core Value Proposition

**All Sorted** is a pre-loaded AI operating system for small business owners. It arrives ready to work on day one — no setup, no onboarding, no configuration. 39 pre-built agents handle your CRM, billing, email, scheduling, content, and security from a single dashboard.

The problem: SMB owners can't hire an operations person ($60k/year), and generic AI tools require months of setup. All Sorted arrives pre-wired for their workflows, their industry, their pain points.

**The message: Everything is sorted. You didn't have to do anything.**

## Key Feature Detail: Security (Built-In, Always-On)

**Guard-dog Scanner**
- Nightly vulnerability scan of all packages and dependencies
- CVE database, VirusTotal (70+ engines), 30+ malicious code patterns
- 6-page dashboard: vulnerabilities, projects affected, audit trails, remediation
- Runs 02:30 GMT nightly, Telegram alerts for critical threats

**Threat Intelligence**
- 6 real-time feeds: NVD, CISA, Bleeping Computer, AbuseIPDB, Shodan, VirusTotal
- Daily digest at 06:15 GMT, real-time critical alerts in dashboard
- 365-day CVE retention, 5-year legal hold on zero-days

**Credential Vault**
- LastPass CLI integration, full encryption
- Per-agent namespace isolation, audit-logged every retrieval
- Never displayed in UI or logs
