Here's what's inside All Sorted, and why it matters for your business.

Productivity

Your whole business on one board.

If you've ever forgotten to follow up with a lead, missed a content deadline, or lost track of where a client is in your pipeline, you know what it costs. Not just money. Time. Attention. Trust.

All Sorted's task board gives you a single kanban view of everything happening in your business. Client tasks, content deadlines, outreach follow-ups, admin work, all of it in one place. It doesn't just sit there waiting for you to add things. It pre-populates with tasks relevant to your business type, and your AI agents keep feeding it as they work.

Nothing falls through the cracks. Because everything is visible, all the time.

  • Pre-loaded with your workflow from day one
  • 7 columns, To Do, In Progress, Waiting, Blocked, Review, Done, and Backlog
  • Smart filtering by label, priority, and agent
  • Auto-populated by your AI agents as they complete work
Security

Always-on protection. Like having Norton for your business.

Every API key, every integration, every dependency in your business stack is scanned every single night. All Sorted's security layer, Guard-dog, runs at 2:30 AM while you sleep, checking your entire system against live threat databases from NVD, CISA, AbuseIPDB, Shodan, VirusTotal, and six other sources.

If anything looks wrong, a known vulnerability, a new CVE affecting software you use, a suspicious package, you get an alert. Critical issues surface immediately. Everything else goes into your morning digest. You never have to think about it, but you're always protected.

The dashboard shows you six views: overview, active vulnerabilities, affected projects, historical reports, full audit trail, and settings. Every scan is logged for 90 days. Critical alerts are kept for a year.

  • Nightly CVE scans against NVD, CISA, Shodan, VirusTotal, and more
  • Automatic severity scoring, critical issues surface immediately
  • Daily digest at 6:30 AM, security summary in your inbox
  • 6-page dashboard: overview, vulnerabilities, projects, reports, audit, settings
API Key Vault
Your keys, encrypted. Every one.

Every API key and credential your business uses is stored in an encrypted vault with per-agent isolation. No key is ever visible in the UI. Every access event is logged with a full audit trail, who accessed what, and when. If you need to rotate a key, it's one update, not a dozen.

Automated Backups
Everything backed up. Every night.

Every night at 2 AM, All Sorted creates a complete compressed backup of your entire workspace. Backups are retained for 30 days. If something ever goes wrong, data corruption, an agent error, a bad deploy, you can restore to any previous night in one step. No cloud service required. No extra cost. Just works.

File Browser
Your entire workspace, in your dashboard.

Browse, inspect, and manage any file in your business workspace directly from the Mission Control UI. No terminal required. Client notes, transcripts, generated blog posts, agent configs, everything is accessible from the same dashboard you use for everything else.

Marketing

A blog that writes itself. SEO that never sleeps.

Most coaches and consultants know they should be publishing content. Almost none of them do it consistently, because writing takes time, SEO takes expertise, and publishing takes systems you haven't built yet.

All Sorted handles all of it. It researches keywords in your niche, writes posts in your voice, generates SEO metadata, and publishes on a schedule, without you touching a word. Every post is designed to rank and to convert. Your blog keeps working while you're with clients.

The SEO monitor tracks your search performance via Google Search Console, clicks, impressions, CTR, position, and surfaces the queries that are actually bringing people to your site. You always know what's working and what to double down on.

  • Keyword research and topic planning, automated
  • Posts written and published on schedule
  • SEO metadata, titles, descriptions, OG tags, generated automatically
  • Google Search Console tracking, top queries, pages, CTR, ranking positions
  • Review queue in your dashboard, approve, edit, or reject before publishing
Social Media

LinkedIn, Instagram, and beyond, handled.

Sunday night content planning is one of the biggest time drains for coaches and personal brands. You know you need to post. You know consistency matters. But by the time you've served clients all week, there's nothing left for content creation.

All Sorted creates content for LinkedIn, Instagram, and other platforms, drafts, captions, hashtags, and scheduling, built around your business and your voice. The LinkedIn agent writes posts and long-form articles. The Instagram agent handles captions, hashtags, Stories, Reels, and Feed posts. Canva integration means visuals are pulled in automatically. Everything is queued in your content calendar.

The Instagram automation goes further: when someone comments a keyword on your post, the system sends them a DM automatically, collecting their email, delivering your lead magnet, and storing the lead. No ManyChat fees. No manual work.

  • LinkedIn posts, articles, and carousels, drafted and queued
  • Instagram Feed, Reels, Stories, captions, hashtags, scheduling
  • Instagram comment-to-DM automation, keyword triggers, email capture, lead delivery
  • Content calendar view, see everything scheduled across platforms
  • Repurpose content across platforms automatically
CRM

Every client. Every conversation. Never lost.

Coaches and consultants run on relationships. But most of them track those relationships in their heads, in a notes app, or in a spreadsheet that's six months out of date. When a client mentions something personal in session, you shouldn't have to hope you'll remember it.

All Sorted's built-in CRM logs every client interaction, tracks where each person is in your pipeline, and triggers follow-ups automatically. After a Zoom session, the mentorship agent watches for the recording, identifies the client by cross-referencing your Google Calendar, transcribes the audio, and generates a structured summary, action items, key moments, client notes, all filed in the client's record.

WhatsApp integration means you can message clients directly from the dashboard, with automated signatures from your AI assistant. Cold outreach runs on its own sequence, prospects move from first contact to qualified lead without you manually following up every time.

  • Full contact records, notes, history, pipeline stage, next action
  • Zoom recording detection, auto-transcription and session summaries
  • WhatsApp integration, message clients, receive replies, track conversations
  • Cold outreach sequences, ICP targeting, AI-written hooks, automated batching
  • Stripe integration, invoice status and payment history in every client record
Reporting

Wake up to a full briefing on your business.

You shouldn't have to log into five different tools to understand what happened while you slept. Every morning, All Sorted sends you a daily summary that covers your entire business, automatically compiled, formatted, and delivered before you start your day.

The report covers tasks completed by your agents overnight, new leads that came in, content that was published, the security digest from Guard-dog's nightly scan, and a financial snapshot from Stripe. If anything requires your attention, it surfaces at the top. If everything is running smoothly, the report confirms it in thirty seconds.

Your agents also deliver their own status updates, each one reporting what it did, what it found, and what (if anything) needs a decision from you. You stay in the picture without having to look.

  • Overnight activity summary, what your agents did while you slept
  • Security digest, Guard-dog scan results and any flags
  • New leads and follow-up status from your outreach pipeline
  • Content published, posts, blog articles, social media
  • Financial snapshot, Stripe balance, recent charges, payout status

Joe documented the architecture in How to Build Your Own AI Agent Operating System, which teaches you to build the whole thing yourself in 3 to 8 months.

All Sorted is the destination for buyers who would rather have it installed.
