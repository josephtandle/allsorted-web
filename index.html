Under the Hood

The architecture from the book. Installed on your machine.

All Sorted is the commercial install of the architecture documented in How to Build Your Own AI Agent Operating System. The book teaches you to build it in 3 to 8 months. All Sorted ships the same system, working, on day one. The dispatch layer, project index, capability routing, durable data, verification, and security stack are the same ones running on MyOS in Bali right now.

Underneath Dispatch sits the relationship graph layer: four live graphs that map your business, your code, your files, and your projects. The newest is the Ontology, which turns every contact, member, and payment scattered across your databases into one resolved identity with an audit trail on every write. Dispatch reads all four graphs as evidence before it routes anything, which is why the system gets sharper the longer it runs. A fifth graph, Graphiti temporal memory, is proving out on MyOS now and lands in the core next.
