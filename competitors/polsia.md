# Polsia — polsia.com

**Tagline:** "AI That Runs Your Company While You Sleep"

## What It Is
Autonomous AI platform that builds and runs companies without human employees. You give it a business idea and vision statement, and it autonomously plans, codes, deploys, markets, and operates the business through coordinated AI agents.

## How It Works
- Daily cycles: specialized AI agents (CEO, Engineer, Growth Manager) wake up overnight, execute tasks, send founder a morning email summary
- Capabilities: write/deploy code, send emails, handle customer support, post on social media, run marketing, analyze metrics, manage investor outreach
- Built on Anthropic's Claude Agent SDK

## How All Sorted Compares
Polsia is a hosted, black-box product for non-technical users who want to type in an idea and have AI do everything. All Sorted is the opposite approach: custom-built, runs on your own hardware, deeply integrated with your actual business tools (Stripe, Zoho, Wix, Notion, WhatsApp, Trello, Slack), and you control every piece.

**What Polsia has that we should consider:**
- Clean daily morning email digest format (we built this: Daily Briefing)
- Simple onboarding flow for non-technical users
- $29-59/mo price point proves market willingness to pay

**What we have that Polsia doesn't:**
- 75+ purpose-built agents vs generic role-based agents
- Real integrations with actual business tools (not simulated)
- Security scanning (Guard Dog, Threat Intelligence)
- Full financial stack (Stripe, Zoho Books, bank account management)
- Trading/prediction markets (Polymarket, MiroFish)
- Health data (WHOOP), smart home, music control
- Open-source component (Golden Claw)
