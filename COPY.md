## Notes for Website Build

- Lead with pain (12 tools, manual work, generic AI) before solution
- The Genie is the most demo-able feature: screenshot or short video of typing a command and watching it execute
- Guard Dog is the most surprising: free, nightly, automatic security scanning that competitors charge thousands for
- The self-evolving machine learning angle is unique and should be prominent
- Pricing note: $20/mo Claude account + $7/mo server. Total $27/mo before any paid integrations. Lead with this.
- Screenshots should be dark-mode, clean, with real data visible (not empty states)
- The "Build Your Own" section appeals to the technical minority but signals credibility to everyone
